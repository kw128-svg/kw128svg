Served as a Teaching Assistant for the undergraduate **Electromagnetism** course at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).

### Responsibilities
* Conducted weekly recitation and problem-solving sessions for second-year physics and science undergraduates.
* Clarified core concepts in electrostatics, magnetostatics, electrodynamics, and Maxwell's equations.
* Graded weekly homework assignments and provided detailed written feedback on students' analytical solutions.
